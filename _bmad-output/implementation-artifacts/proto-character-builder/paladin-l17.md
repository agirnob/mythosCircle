## Narrative
- **Appearance** — His plate armor is encrusted with salt and barnacles, weeping brine from every joint. A heavy cloak of kelp-woven silk clings to his shoulders like wet seaweed.
- **Personality** — He speaks with the crushing weight of the deep ocean, calm yet relentlessly inevitable. He views all life as a temporary bubble destined to pop beneath the waves.
- **Background** — Once a celebrated captain of the harbor guard, he survived a shipwreck by making a pact with the entity slumbering in the abyss.
- **Goals** — To ensure the rising tides reclaim the corrupt spires of the city to appease his master.
- **Secret** — He is slowly turning into salt and coral from the inside out.
- **Rumor** — They say if you press your ear to his breastplate, you can hear the crushing pressure of the deep sea.
- **Party hook** — He offers the party passage through the treacherous reef if they can recover a lost relic from a sunken cathedral.
- **Voice style** — A low, bubbling rasp that sounds like stones grinding under heavy currents.
- **Catchphrases** — The tide claims all.

**World integration**
- Reputation — A grim sentinel of the docks, feared by smugglers and respected by the desperate.
- Current location — A salt-stained chapel built into the rotting pier of the lower docks.
- Reaction matrix — Hostile to those who pollute the harbor, indifferent to the law, and protective of the drowning.
