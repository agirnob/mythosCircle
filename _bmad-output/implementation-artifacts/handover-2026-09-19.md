## The graph feature (shipped contract)

- Route `/campaigns/:id/graph` (`?focus=` optional deep link), auth-guarded;
  entry points: per-entity "See web" + "Open graph" nav in WorldView.
- **Whole world always**: no cap/truncation UI; HUD shows rendered counts.
- **Cluster layout** (`clusterLayout` in
  `frontend/src/components/graph/graphModel.ts`): (1) skeleton — factions +
  unaffiliated via seeded FR; (2) fill — members on a deterministic ring
  around their faction, associates just outside; (3) reflect refinement —
  edges attract, pairs repel, centroid gravity bounds drift, member anchor
  springs; (4) reach-aware faction separation (each faction claims ring +
  nested depth); (5) order-preserving angular redistribution per ring; (6)
  bounded ambient de-overlap (ring members never move).
- **Focus**: highlight = focus + 1-hop (both directions, committed types)
  with labels; dim rest; click node refocuses (`?focus=` replaces, NO
  relayout/re-fit — pinned by test); empty canvas clears; hover tooltip
  `source → type(counter) → target`; labels toggle (default OFF); kind +
  relationship filters; zoom/fit/reset/keys/touch; drag-guarded refocus;
  pane-drag never clears focus; raw ULIDs never in UI (breadcrumb
  human-readable).
- **Known data-shape limit** (documented in spec-7-1 change log): a faction
  that is itself `member_of` another faction puts 2/17 of Drowned Harbor's
  memberships near the grandparent — semantically inside its own faction's
  area; not a rendering defect.
