## What shipped

   DM-owned custom style text) as optional `image` job payload keys:
   - Closed vocab `style ∈ {photorealistic, cartoonish, illustration,
     custom}`, `framing ∈ {portrait, headshot, full_body}`,
     `background ∈ {scene, plain, dark, transparent}`;
     `custom_style` (free text) REQUIRES `style=custom` (canonicalize,
     never silently drop). Unknown keys/values → 422 at enqueue AND the
     runner's re-check (same `portrait_options` helper).

     drafts persist per entity (the DM-typed-generation discipline);
     portraits are DM-triggered only (2026-09-15: the accept
     auto-enqueue was removed).
